# Submission notes — write these yourself

Your AI assistant is not allowed to write or edit this file. A few plain sentences per heading
is enough; the interviewer uses them to start the discussion.

## Why this stack

## Synchronization

one wall? How does a snapshot stay internally consistent while bursts are happening?

## Observed bottleneck

What limited throughput or p99 in your measurements, and how did you tell?

## One optimization you measured

What you changed, and the before/after numbers from the grader.

## What you had the AI write

A short list: which parts of the code came from the assistant, and which you wrote or
rewrote by hand.
